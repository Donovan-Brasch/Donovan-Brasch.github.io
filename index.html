    Projects

    Back o' House

    A home for every show you've ever been to.

    Between working concerts and my passion for live music, I've been to too many shows to keep track of, so I built a site that catalogues and ranks your shows, links their setlists and media, lets you share with friends, and finds what's coming up nearby. For now it's mostly a community of my friends and me, but I'd love for anyone who thinks it's cool to join!

    Visit Back o' House

    Parkcast

    A tool for landing a national park campsite.

    I've always had a tough time knowing how far in advance I have to book a campsite, so I built a tool that forecasts booking odds for 148 campgrounds across 39 national parks. It also shows crowds and weather for all 63 parks, so you can find the best time to take your trip.

    Try Parkcast
